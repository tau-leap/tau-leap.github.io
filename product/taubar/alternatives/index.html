TauBar and its alternatives: window-level taskbars for Mac

TauBar requires macOS 26 (Tahoe) or later on Apple Silicon. If your Mac doesn't match, skip to the alternatives below. Several of them will run on it.

TauBar adds a window-level taskbar to the bottom of your Mac. This page compares it with four apps people often weigh it against: uBar, Tungsten Edge, Winstrix and DockDoor. We make TauBar, so read this with that in mind. Every competitor fact links to the vendor's own page and was checked on 2026-11-12. Prices and versions change, so check the linked page before you buy.

Who TauBar is NOT for

  • Intel Macs, or macOS 15 and earlier. TauBar won't help you. Look at Tungsten Edge (macOS 12+, Intel and Apple silicon), Winstrix (macOS 14+, Universal) or DockDoor (macOS 13+, Apple silicon and Intel). The current uBar 5 has the same requirement as TauBar: macOS 26 and Apple Silicon.
  • You want open source. TauBar is closed source. Tungsten Edge and DockDoor are both GPL-3.0 on GitHub.
  • You'd rather keep the Dock and just add window previews. DockDoor does exactly that, and it's free.
  • You want the whole Mac to behave like Windows. That means Windows shortcuts, scrolling, window snapping and clipboard history. Winstrix is built for that. TauBar only adds a taskbar, and it doesn't tile windows.
  • You want maximum configurability. uBar offers a dock mode, multiple rows, any screen edge, themes, and commercial licensing for teams. It has been refined over many years.

What TauBar is for

The Dock groups everything by app. TauBar gives each open window its own item on a bar at the bottom of the screen, with its title. With three Chrome profiles or several Finder windows open, you click the one you mean. Chrome profiles are shown. The free plan also includes pinned apps, a Start button for launching apps and search, a clock with a month calendar, multiple displays and auto-hide. You can use it alongside the Dock. There's no account and no usage telemetry.

Where the others are better (honestly)

  • uBar is the most mature and most configurable. It can run as a taskbar or a dock, on any edge, up to five rows tall, with a separate bar per monitor and themes. It also has commercial and enterprise licensing.
  • Tungsten Edge is free while in beta, open source, and runs on macOS 12 and later on Intel and Apple silicon. It doesn't need Screen Recording permission, and you can install it with brew install --cask tungsten-edge.
  • Winstrix costs less than TauBar's lifetime license ($14.99 one-time) and goes much further if you're coming from Windows. It adds shortcuts, Alt+Tab, window snapping, clipboard history and Windows-style scrolling.
  • DockDoor is free and open source, with no paywall, and it keeps the native Dock. If hover previews and a window switcher are enough for you, you may not need a taskbar at all.

Where TauBar fits

  • You're on macOS 26+ with Apple Silicon and want each window on a bar at the bottom, with its title.
  • You want a free core taskbar after the trial. Pro is optional: monthly, yearly or a $19.99 lifetime license.
  • You want a Start button, a clock with a calendar, pinned apps and Chrome profiles on the same bar.
  • You care about permissions. TauBar has no account and no usage telemetry. It uses Accessibility only to identify and focus windows. Screen Recording is needed only if you turn on preview thumbnails.

Alternatives to uBar on Mac

uBar 5 now requires macOS 26 and Apple Silicon. If you're looking for a uBar alternative:

  • On an Intel Mac or an older macOS: try Tungsten Edge (macOS 12+) or Winstrix (macOS 14+). If previews on the existing Dock are enough, try DockDoor (macOS 13+).
  • On macOS 26+ with Apple Silicon and you want a free core taskbar or a cheaper lifetime license: try TauBar ($19.99 lifetime, or free core after the trial) or Winstrix ($14.99).
  • You want open source: try Tungsten Edge or DockDoor.

FAQ

Does TauBar work on Intel Macs or macOS 15?

No. TauBar requires macOS 26 (Tahoe) or later on Apple Silicon. On an Intel Mac or an older macOS, look at Tungsten Edge (macOS 12+), DockDoor (macOS 13+) or Winstrix (macOS 14+).

Is TauBar free?

Every feature is free for 10 days. After that, the core taskbar stays free: window switching, pinning, the Start menu, the clock and calendar, and multiple displays. Pro costs $0.99/month, $9.99/year or $19.99 lifetime, on up to 2 Macs.

Does TauBar replace the Dock?

You don't have to choose. We recommend running it alongside the Dock. You can also move the Dock to the left or right and make the taskbar your home base.

Is TauBar open source?

No. If open source matters to you, Tungsten Edge and DockDoor are both GPL-3.0.

How is TauBar different from DockDoor?

DockDoor adds window previews and an ⌥Tab switcher to the native Dock. TauBar adds a separate bar at the bottom of the screen, where each window has its own titled item.
