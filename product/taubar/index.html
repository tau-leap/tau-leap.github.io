For Windows users on Mac

The taskbar you know, now on Mac.

Miss switching by window title? TauBar brings a Windows-style taskbar to MacOS.

  • No account required
  • Apple Silicon
  • MacOS Tahoe 26+

Switch by window — not just by app

Dock groups everything by app. TauBar puts each open window on the bar, so you can find it by its title. Chrome profiles are shown too.

Pin apps to the taskbar

Pin apps on the right too. Reach them the moment the idea hits.

System shortcuts on the bar

Launch Siri, Spotlight, Mission Control, and Show Desktop from icons on the taskbar.

Start menu where you expect it

Launch apps, search, and open settings from the Start button — right on the taskbar, where you left it on Windows.

Active window highlight

A clear border marks the focused window, so you always know which one is active.

Clock & calendar

Check the time and events from the clock, and see your next event in the menu bar — no other app needed.

Media & system controls

Adjust volume, brightness, and media playback right from the tray.

Window Switcher

Press ⌥Tab — like Alt+Tab — to browse every window across every Space, with titles and previews.

⌥+Tab

Temporary storage

Park files on the taskbar while you switch apps or Spaces, then drop them where you need them.

⌂

TauBar has no usage telemetry. Accessibility is used only to identify and focus windows. Screen Recording is needed only when you choose to show preview thumbnails.

FAQ

Good to know

Key points for people who switched from Windows.

Does TauBar replace the Dock?

We recommend using it alongside the Dock. Or hide the Dock on the left or right and make the taskbar your home base.

Why are Accessibility permissions needed?

MacOS requires this permission for TauBar to read window titles, focus windows, and restore minimized windows.

Is Screen Recording required?

Only for preview thumbnails in Window Switcher. Without it, TauBar shows app icons and titles instead.

What happens after the trial?

After 10 days you stay on Free with the core taskbar. Pro features need Monthly, Yearly, or Lifetime. Your preferences remain saved on your Mac.

What is included in Free vs Pro?

Free covers switching, pinning, Start menu, and clock. Pro adds Window Switcher, drag shelf, calendar events, media controls, badges, and more — see Pricing.

How many Macs can I use?

Each license can be activated on up to two Macs for your personal use.

Does TauBar work with multiple displays?

Yes. The taskbar follows your display setup, and Window Switcher opens on the display under your pointer.
