Privacy Policy

TauBar stores your settings and pinned apps on your Mac only. We do not collect usage analytics inside the app.

When you purchase a license, your email and payment are processed by Stripe. License activation sends your license key and a device identifier to our license server for validation.

Feature requests on our feedback board are governed by that service's privacy policy. Optional tip payments are processed by Stripe.

Contact: support@taubar.app
