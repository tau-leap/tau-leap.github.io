Features

Compare, then decide

A diff tool that lets you act on the result.

Side by side

VS Code-style colours for added and removed lines. An overview gutter shows where the diffs are.

Copy a block across

Send the selected hunk left-to-right or right-to-left. Both panes stay editable.

Drop files in

One file fills the left pane, the next fills the right. Finder or launch arguments work too.

Encodings kept intact

UTF-8, Shift_JIS, EUC-JP and more are detected on open and preserved on save.

TauDiff never contacts a server. The files you compare stay on your computer.
