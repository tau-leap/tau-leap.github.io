Features

What you need, nothing else

The editing basics, kept small and local.

Multi-tab editing

Open several files at once. Drag and drop onto the window. Session restore brings your tabs back on the next launch.

Encoding & line endings

UTF-8 and Shift_JIS, LF and CRLF — detected on open, chosen on save.

Find, replace, project search

Search the current file or every open tab. Regular expressions, jump list, next/previous.

Markdown preview

For .md files, open a GFM preview beside the editor when you want it.

TauPad has no analytics, no account and no network of its own. Documents stay where you saved them.

FAQ

Good to know

A short briefing before you download.

Does TauPad sync to the cloud?

No. It reads and writes local files.

Which encodings are supported?

UTF-8 and Shift_JIS.

Is there a Windows version?

Not yet. A Windows build is planned.

Where are settings stored?

~/Library/Application Support/com.taupad.desktop/settings.json

Is TauPad free?

Yes. Download it from GitHub Releases.
